History

Boblett Brothers

Boblett Brothers no longer hauls freight. This page is only a history of the company.

Chad Boblett founded Boblett Brothers LLC in 2011 in Lexington, Kentucky. He started the company after ten years in the Marine Corps, including a tour in Iraq, and a few years as a professional driver. He bought a used truck on credit cards.

He named the company Boblett Brothers in honor of his fellow truckers. The company ran van loads and power-only trailer repositioning for more than 100 shippers and freight brokers. It was where Rate Per Mile Masters started.
