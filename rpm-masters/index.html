Chad Boblett's community for carriers

Rate Per Mile Masters

RPM Masters stands for Rate Per Mile Masters. It is a private Facebook group for owner-operators, carriers, brokers and agents. Joining Rate Per Mile Masters means joining this group.

Join RPM Masters on Facebook

The group

Chad reviews posts and keeps the discussion professional. Chad's content covers rates, fuel, and the freight market for carriers.

How it started

Rate Per Mile Masters started out of Chad's trucking company, Boblett Brothers. Chad has mentored carriers through the group since March 2014.

Membership

The Freight Pod reported about 36,000 to 37,000 members as of December 2024.

A past session
